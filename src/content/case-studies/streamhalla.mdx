## The product

[StreamHalla](https://streamhalla.com) is your stream analyst: a VOD analytics tool for Twitch, YouTube, and Kick streamers. Paste a public VOD link and get your stream reviewed, with five evidence-backed recommendations for your next stream. It reads the chat and listens to the broadcast itself, and it never downloads or stores your stream.

It exists because the native dashboards don't answer the question that actually matters. Twitch Analytics and YouTube Studio show view counts; they don't explain why viewers left or which moments resonated, and they don't give you any tips to improve. Streamers trying to grow are left guessing. StreamHalla replaces the guessing with a read on what happened and what to do about it.

## What it does

**It listens to the stream.** Audio analysis follows your pacing, your dead air, how often you address the audience, whether you answered chat, and which topics the room responded to.

**A sentiment timeline.** See which moments made your stream pop off and which ones stalled it, with the moments worth clipping or going back to flagged for you.

**A coaching plan in every review.** Each review ends with a game plan and tips for your next stream: five evidence-backed recommendations.

**Scored against you.** Scores are only there to benchmark you against yourself. Your next stream is measured against your last ones, not somebody else's channel.

**Streamer Trends.** Finds what draws your audience to you, and how your content, your schedule and your timing move your numbers: how long to stream, which days work best, what time to start, and which topics land.

**Daily auto-monitoring.** On Pro, StreamHalla monitors your channels, reviews every new stream, and sends you the link when it's ready.

StreamHalla works across Twitch, YouTube, and Kick, on any public VOD.
